Simulate exclusive electron–proton production of neutral mesons and axion-like particles. Retain exact three-body kinematics and apply detector selections for Electron–Ion Collider studies.

Particle evolution in the plasma

Metastable-dynamics

Trace charged pions, muons and kaons injected by long-lived-particle decays into the MeV primordial plasma. Solve their number-density evolution with decays, annihilations and nucleon interactions.

Particle abundances · Neutrino-solver inputs

Mathematica · FeynCalc

RepositoryPaperZenodo
Sterile-neutrino dark matter

sterile-dm-lfa

Calculate sterile-neutrino dark-matter production in the presence of primordial lepton flavor asymmetries. Compare a Python discretization solver with a Mathematica analytic solver, and obtain relic abundances and momentum spectra.

Relic abundance · Momentum spectra · Flavor asymmetries

Python · Mathematica

RepositoryExample notebookPaper
Neutrino Boltzmann solver

Nudec_LLP_Solver

Solve the momentum-dependent neutrino Boltzmann equations for long-lived-particle decays in the MeV primordial plasma. Follow neutrino spectra and plasma evolution, incorporating the decay histories of injected muons and pions from Metastable-dynamics.

Neutrino spectra · Plasma evolution
